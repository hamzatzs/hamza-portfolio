AUTODIDACT: a self-improving robot learning loop

  • Erlangen, 2026
  • Team of five
  • Built in 48 hours
  • Semi-finalist

An autonomous learning loop for a simulated cath-lab robot, using a MuJoCo digital twin of the FR3 Duo. It finds gaps in its training data, requests targeted demonstrations, retrains, and promotes a new policy only when a statistical test shows it is better.

Final-year project, B.Sc. Mechanical Engineering

Bat-inspired micro UAV

GIKI, Pakistan | 2019–2023

A biologically inspired micro aerial vehicle for potential disaster-response and surveillance use. I designed a four-degree-of-freedom wing mechanism (flapping, morphing and twisting) to replicate aspects of bat-wing motion, 3D-printed lightweight components, and ran CFD and FEA in ANSYS, COMSOL and MATLAB.

Wing dynamics were simulated in MSC Adams, a microcontroller-based control system adjusted the wing in real time, and a scaled prototype was validated in wind-tunnel and field tests.
